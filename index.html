How It Works

A transparent, structured four-step feedback mechanism that turns student insights into actionable campus enhancements.

01

Give Feedback

Complete structured questionnaires assigned by faculty and admin with criteria ratings and constructive remarks.

02

Identify Issues

AI analytics and automated taggers categorize complaints, recurring bottlenecks, and departmental improvement areas.

03

Take Action

Department heads and campus administration assign tasks, allocate resources, and resolve student grievances.

04

See Improvement

Track tangible semester-over-semester score growth, facility upgrades, and published resolution reports.

Platform Capabilities

Built for Modern Campus Governance

Everything college administrators, professors, and students need to collaborate on educational excellence.

Feedback

Structured course reviews, faculty evaluations, and institutional assessment forms with flexible scoring criteria.

Complaints

Secure grievance lodging for infrastructure, hostel, mess, and academics with priority categorization and SLA tracking.

Requests

Formal service requests for library resources, lab equipment, special accommodations, and administrative certificates.

Track Issues

Real-time timeline visibility on ticket statuses: Submitted, In Review, In Progress, and Resolved with officer notes.

Privacy

Role-based access control and optional double-blind anonymity ensure students can express candid, honest feedback safely.

Improvements

AI-assisted sentiment trends, faculty self-improvement recommendations, and executive analytics reports.

Transparent Campus Governance

Public Grievance Resolution Portal

High-priority and long-pending campus grievances are automatically published for community transparency. Zero student PII is exposed.
